# Flipkart × Autopilot: Andheri East intro film

Draft 2, 23 Nov 2026. About 115 seconds, 1920×1080, 24 fps.

## What changes from the first draft

- **It opens on Flipkart's question, not on us.** "Your next workspace starts with the journey to it" is a slogan. The client is choosing between three buildings. So the film states the decision, then answers it building by building.
- **One reason per building, said once, shown on screen.**
  - Privilon: the metro at the door.
  - Corporate Avenue: a ready, furnished campus.
  - 723 Avenue: the top of the building, ready now.
  The first draft gave each building three or four soft benefits.
- **Every figure is from the deck or the measured route data.** Nothing is promised that is not confirmed: no expansion space, no exclusive terrace rights, no views.
- **The light story is real.** Privilon's orientation was verified on Google satellite, and the sun was computed for Marol in late November. Its long east face takes the 7 a.m. sun and its west face the 5:30 p.m. sun. We can show both because both are true.
- **2D carries the substance.** Each building's segment ends on its real CAD plans, animated: lines draw in, zones fill, the seat count counts up. There are no 3D interiors.

## Production

**Picture: no browser screen captures.** The first cut looked cartoonish because it filmed the web explorer.
- **Exteriors:** rendered in Blender Cycles, as real camera moves (push-in, rise, drone orbit), 5 to 8 s each.
  - Poly Haven CC0 HDRI skies, the computed sun for sunrise and sunset, and lit interiors at dusk.
  - Hero buildings get modelled depth: fins, slab bands, recessed bases.
- **Context within 100 m of each shot:** upgraded first.
  - Real depth on facades: displaced window reveals, balcony slabs, AC units.
  - Scanned CC0 materials, and grime in the roughness.
  - Better trees and cars, or kept out of frame.
- **Clouds:** volumetric cloud descent, as in the Reliable film, re-rendered with the new sky.
- **2D:** the real CAD plans as animated vector drawings, with lines drawing in, zones filling and counters, plus the micro-market map with the Line 1 and Line 3 routes. Built as HyperFrames compositions.

**Voice.** Three short auditions, 15 seconds each, before the full read:
- Lisa, the warm Indian English voice from the Reliable film;
- a male North Indian English voice, energetic and conversational;
- one alternative female voice.

The founder picks one. Pacing has breaths around every figure.

**Sound.**
- A licensed cinematic music bed: piano and strings over a soft pulse, building at the close.
- Sound design: cloud wind, city ambience under the drone shots, a train pass at Marol Naka, soft UI ticks on the CAD counters, a riser into each building.
- The music ducks under the voice.

**Grade.** One look for the whole film: AgX, gentle contrast, fine grain, subtle bloom on lit windows, letterbox-free.

**Render budget, Apple M5 GPU.**
- About 20 s per frame at 1080p with denoise.
- Twelve shots of about 6 s each, so about 1,700 frames, or about 9 to 10 hours. It runs overnight.
- 2D and audio take a few hours.
