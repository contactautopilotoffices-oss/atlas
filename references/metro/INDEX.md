# FACTS.md — confirmed technical facts

Source: Wikipedia, "Purple Line (Namma Metro)" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purple_Line_(Namma_Metro)
(fetched 2026-08-22; facts cross-checked against BMRCL-derived station table in the same article).

1. **Electrification: 750 V DC third rail — NO overhead catenary.** Confirmed. Infobox: "Electrification: 750 V DC third rail"; Rolling stock section: trains "operate on 750 volt direct current with third rail power collection". Traction: four 180 kW motors per motor coach. Same system-wide (Phase 1 & 2 BEML stock).
2. **Track gauge:** 1,435 mm standard gauge.
3. **Viaduct/pier style:** Mostly elevated line (31 elevated stations of 37). Elevated deck is precast segmental box girder on single central piers with flared pier caps in the road median (see photos 05, 08). Whitefield extension (Reaches 1A/1B) built by Italian-Thai Development; contracts awarded May 2017 (~₹1,300 crore both packages); construction began Feb 2018.
4. **Stations on the Whitefield extension (KR Pura → Whitefield), all elevated, side platforms, all opened 26 March 2023:**
   Krishnarajapura (K.R. Pura), Singayyanapalya, Garudacharpalya, Hoodi, Seetharamapalya, Kundalahalli, Nallurhalli, Sri Sathya Sai Hospital, Pattandur Agrahara, Kadugodi Tree Park, Hopefarm Channasandra, Whitefield (Kadugodi). Benniganahalli (between Baiyappanahalli and KR Pura) opened 9 October 2023.
5. **Opening dates:** KR Pura–Kundalahalli (Reach 1A, 6.5 km) and Kundalahalli–Whitefield/Kadugodi (Reach 1B, 7.21 km) both opened **26 March 2023** (inaugurated by PM Modi 25 March 2023 per cited Deccan Herald source). Baiyappanahalli–KR Pura gap (with Benniganahalli) opened **9 October 2023**, making the full line Whitefield (Kadugodi)–Challaghatta (43.49 km, 37 stations) operational.
6. **Depot for the Whitefield end:** Kadugodi Depot.
7. **Train dimensions (modelling):** BEML/Hyundai Rotem stainless-steel coaches, 20.8 m long × 2.88 m wide × 3.8 m high; 6-car sets; max speed 80 km/h (operating ~36–40 km/h).
8. **Platform screen doors:** Namma Metro elevated Purple Line stations have half-height platform screen doors (visible in photos 02, 03, 11).
9. **Speeds/geometry:** commercial speed 67.5 km/h on straights, 35 km/h on curves, 45 km/h in stations (RDSO) — useful for curve radii plausibility.

Secondary source cited in the article for the Whitefield inauguration:
- Deccan Herald, 25 Mar 2023: https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/pm-inaugurates-much-awaited-whitefield-metro-line-in-bengaluru-1205270.html (as cited on Wikipedia; not independently re-fetched)
