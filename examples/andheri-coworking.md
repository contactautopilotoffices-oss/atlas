## Why each centre scores as it does

- **Rush Co-works · Boston House** (value): The smallest centre visited, at a mid price for a B building beside WEH metro. Only two small cabins free.
- **Innov8 · Boston House** (premium): Second Innov8 site on this list, in the same building as Rush. Rs 2,000 a seat dearer than Rush for the same desk size; 12 seats free.
- **Executive Spaces · Rustomjee Central Park** (value): The cheapest seat visited, Rs 14,000, in the best-graded building of the WEH cluster. Sold as a managed office, so vacancy, hours and parking were not shared on the visit. Rustomjee Central Park itself is not in Autopilot's proposed inventory; only this competitor is shown.
- **Executive Spaces · Chandak Chambers** (value): The cheapest of the WEH cluster, at the foot of the metro station. Three cabins of 5 to 10 seats free; the smallest desk size on the list.
- **Quest Coworks · Technopolis Knowledge Park** (scale): The biggest block of vacancy visited: a 150-seat cabin plus smaller ones, in a campus with a gym and pool. Mid price, but the furthest of the WEH group from a station.
- **Community Coworks · Akruti Trade Centre (MIDC Road 7)** (value): The largest desk on the list (4 x 2 ft) at one of the lower prices. Four cabins of 5 to 16 seats free. The visit sheet wrote 'Akruti Trade Star'; every public source puts this centre at Ackruti Trade Centre on MIDC Road 7, which also fits the sheet's 'MIDC and Chakala' metro note. The sheet's note of a 16,000 sq ft Cognizant managed office here is unverified.
- **WeWork · K Raheja Platinum** (premium): The price ceiling and the only Grade A building visited: Rs 28,000 a seat, 300 seats free, the dearest parking, and a five-day week. Sets what the top of the Marol market pays.
- **Innov8 · Zion House** (premium): Innov8's flagship on the highway in Vile Parle East: 1,500 seats, the second-highest price, and nothing larger than a 20-seat cabin free. Outside Andheri East proper but competing for the same WEH tenants.
- **Smartworks · Fleet House** (scale): 2,500 seats at the Marol Naka interchange with 100 seats free in blocks of 6 to 50. The nearest large centre to the Privilon and 723 Avenue sites.
- **Innov8 · Akruti Trade Star** (premium): Innov8's third site on the list, opened in 2026 beside Chakala metro: Rs 20,000 for a 3 x 2 ft desk, 26 seats free. Executive Spaces announced a 650-seat centre in the same building in March 2026. The visit sheet names Marol as the metro; the building stands at Chakala (J B Nagar).
- **315Work Avenue · Silver Utopia** (value): Small and nearly full: 10 seats free of 240, with two large-corporate clients. Mid price, but the second-dearest parking visited.
- **Awfis · Skyline Icon** (value): The listed operator's Marol centre at the median price, 350 m from the interchange, with fintech tenants and 36 seats free.

## What the visit found

- **Centres visited.** 12 centres by 9 operators across 11 buildings, 11 in Andheri East and 1 in Vile Parle East on the Western Express Highway (as of 2026-10, high confidence). Includes Executive Spaces at Rustomjee Central Park as a competitor; the building itself is not in the proposed inventory. Visited_Co-working_Space.xlsx (site visits, October 2026).
- **Price per seat, quoted on the visit.** Rs 14,000 to 28,000 a month plus GST; median Rs 18,000 (as of 2026-10, high confidence). Eight of the 12 sit between Rs 14,000 and 18,000; Executive Spaces at Rustomjee Central Park is the floor at Rs 14,000. The three above Rs 20,000 are WeWork (28,000), Innov8 Zion House (26,000) and Smartworks Fleet House (23,000).
- **Seats in the 11 centres.** About 11,155 seats in total; three centres hold 7,300 of them (WeWork 3,000, Smartworks 2,500, Quest 1,800) (as of 2026-10, medium confidence). Seat totals as told to the team on the visit; the operators' own published figures differ for several centres (see Published prices and capacity).
- **Vacant seats counted.** About 800 seats free across the 11 centres that shared it (Rustomjee did not); WeWork (300), Quest (about 200) and Smartworks (100) hold three quarters of it (as of 2026-10, medium confidence). Counted from the cabins and desks named on the visit. 'Some small cabins' at Quest and the mix at WeWork are not counted beyond the stated numbers.
- **Desk sizes.** 3 x 2 ft at Rush, Innov8 Boston House, both Executive Spaces centres and Innov8 Trade Star; 3.5 x 2 ft at Quest, 315Work and Awfis; 4 x 2 ft at Community Coworks, WeWork, Innov8 Zion and Smartworks (as of 2026-10, high confidence). The 4 x 2 ft desk at Community Coworks (Rs 16,500) is the cheapest large desk visited.
- **Grade.** Only WeWork at K Raheja Platinum is in a building the team graded A; Rustomjee Central Park is A-; the other ten are B (as of 2026-10, high confidence). Grades as the team recorded them on the visit.
- **Hours.** 9 am to 9 pm Monday to Saturday at nine centres; Quest 8 am to 8 pm; WeWork Monday to Friday only; not shared at Rustomjee Central Park (as of 2026-10, high confidence).
- **Car parking, a month.** Rs 3,500 (Innov8 Zion) to Rs 8,000 (WeWork); Rs 4,000 to 5,000 at most centres; not shared at Innov8 Trade Star and Awfis (as of 2026-10, high confidence). Two-wheeler parking Rs 1,500 to 3,000 where offered; none at Innov8 Zion.
- **Amenities.** Every centre offered the standard package plus breakout and printing; Quest and WeWork add a gym, play zone and swimming pool access (as of 2026-10, high confidence).
- **Same building, two prices.** Boston House: Rush Rs 18,000 and Innov8 Rs 20,000 for the same 3 x 2 ft desk (as of 2026-10, high confidence). Innov8 is on the 7th floor and Rush on the 5th (tenant and operator pages).
- **Cognizant managed office.** The visit sheet notes a 16,000 sq ft managed office for CTS India run by Community Coworks (as of 2026-10, low confidence). Not found in any public source on 23 Oct 2026; the only public 16,000 sq ft figure is the size listing sites give for the Community Coworks centre itself. Treat as the team's note until confirmed.

[Source](https://www.aprea.asia/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Press-Release-Mumbai-H1-2026.pdf). Knight Frank. JP Morgan's 2.2 million sq ft Powai lease was about 31% of the half-year. Flex operators took 0.44 million sq ft of it.
