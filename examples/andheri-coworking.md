## What the sheet shows

- **Centres visited.** 16 centres by 11 operators across 15 buildings: 15 in Andheri East and 1 in Vile Parle on the Western Express Highway. Boston House holds two (Rush Co-works and Innov8). Innov8 runs four of the 16. Visited_Co-working_Space.xlsx (site visits, October 2026).
- **Per seat cost.** Rs 12,000 (Teloz Space, AJ House) to Rs 28,000 (WeWork, K Raheja Platinum) a month; median Rs 18,000. As quoted on the sheet, most plus GST, with DevX corrected to Rs 18,000. Twelve of the 16 sit between Rs 14,000 and 20,000.
- **Seats.** About 9,400 seats across the 16 centres; WeWork (3,000), Quest (1,800) and Innov8 Zion House (1,500) hold 6,300 of them. Innov8 Solitaire is given as 200+.
- **Dedicated and flex desks.** Dedicated desks Rs 10,000 (Teloz) to Rs 22,000 (Innov8 Zion House); flex seats Rs 10,000 to 21,000 where offered. From the sheet's open and dedicated desk row. Rush Co-works and Executive Spaces Chandak Chambers offer no flex seats.
- **Hours and extra time.** Most run 9 am to 9 pm, Monday to Saturday. Seven centres allow no work after hours; the rest charge Rs 200 per seat to Rs 6,500 an hour. Sunday use: not offered at three Innov8 centres; Rs 1,000 to Rs 6,500 an hour elsewhere.
- **Meeting rooms.** From about Rs 500 an hour (4 to 5 seats) to Rs 4,000 an hour (10 seats at WeWork). Plus GST, from the sheet's meeting room row.
- **Car parking, a month.** Rs 3,500 (Innov8 Zion House) to Rs 8,000 (WeWork); Rs 4,000 to 6,000 at most centres. To be confirmed at Innov8 Trade Star; not shared for the Executive Spaces managed office.
- **Grade.** Five centres are in buildings the team graded A or A-: WeWork Raheja Platinum, Innov8 Solitaire, 91Springboard 74 Technopark, DevX Dynasty and Executive Spaces Rustomjee (A-). The other eleven are B.
- **Nearest to the proposed sites.** To Privilon: Teloz AJ House 500 m, 315work Silver Utopia 600 m, WeWork 850 m. To Corporate Avenue: Quest Technopolis 400 m, Executive Spaces Rustomjee 800 m. Distances as written on the sheet.
- **Managed office for CTS India.** The sheet notes Community Coworks runs a 16,000 sq ft carpet managed office used by CTS India.

## The operators

- **WeWork India.** Listed on NSE and BSE on 10 Oct 2025. At 31 Mar 2026: 76 centres, 8.6 million sq ft, 8 cities, 126,900 desks, occupancy 86.9%; Mumbai 14 centres, 1.5 million sq ft, 19,400 desks. FY26 revenue Rs 2,477 crore, PAT Rs 179 crore. [Source](https://wework.co.in/investors-relations/WeWork%20-%20Media%20Release%20Q4.pdf). Q1 FY27 (Jun 2026): revenue Rs 698 crore, 79 centres, 133,600 desks, occupancy 84.9%, mature centres 87.5%. Embassy Group is the promoter.
- **Innov8.** Part of the OYO group (now PRISM) since 2019. FY26 revenue Rs 201 crore (+76%), PAT Rs 13.7 crore; 58 centres at 31 Mar 2026, 72 by Jul 2026, target 101 in FY27; about 24,000 desks across 1.2 million sq ft (Sep 2025). [Source](https://inc42.com/buzz/innov8s-fy26-profit-zooms-multifold-to-%E2%82%B913-7-cr-revenue-crosses-%E2%82%B9200-cr-mark/). Lists nine Andheri East centres on its site. Three of the 11 centres on this atlas are Innov8, at three price points: Rs 20,000, 20,000 and 26,000.
- **315Work Avenue.** Founded 2019 in Bengaluru by Manas Mehrotra; about 3 million sq ft and 60,000 seats across 45 locations in five cities (Mar 2025), with a plan to add 2 million sq ft in FY26 for about Rs 400 crore. [Source](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/315work-avenue-plans-to-add-2-mn-sq-ft-in-fy26-invest-rs-400-crore-125032501052_1.html). Two addresses in the Mumbai region on its site: Silver Utopia and Wagle Estate, Thane. Positions itself for teams of 25 and above.
- **Quest Coworks.** Mumbai company incorporated 2013 (founder Prasad Shinde). In March 2026 it bought 57,567 sq ft and 51 car parks at Akruti Softech Park, MIDC Marol, from Capgemini for Rs 55.84 crore, to expand in Andheri. [Source](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/capgemini-sells-five-andheri-office-assets-to-quest-coworks-for-56-cr-126031900153_1.html). A second Quest centre is coming to Andheri East, owned rather than leased.
- **Executive Spaces.** Mumbai-only operator, first centre July 2019 (Goregaon East). Its site lists five centres, two of them on this atlas (Rustomjee Central Park and Chandak Chambers); on 31 Mar 2026 it announced a ninth, 650-plus seats at Trade Star, Andheri East, next to Chakala metro. [Source](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/executive-space_executivespaces-officeforrent-officespaceavailable-activity-7444598351532040192-FkF-). That puts Executive Spaces in Trade Star alongside Innov8, with more seats than either Innov8 or Community Coworks reported there.
- **Community Coworks.** Founded 2019, Mumbai; '1,000+ members, 50+ companies, 50,000+ sq ft'. Two Andheri East centres: Ackruti Trade Centre (MIDC) and Solitaire Corporate Park (Chakala). [Source](https://www.communityspaces.co.in/).
- **Rush Co-works.** Founded 2021, Mumbai (Capt. Ashish Chandna). Centres at Godrej Two (Vikhroli), Mayuresh Square and Boston House; four more announced in Navi Mumbai and Pune. [Source](https://www.rushcoworks.com/). Boston House was listed as Inspire Cospaces before the rebrand; some listing sites still use that name.

[Source](https://www.aprea.asia/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Press-Release-Mumbai-H1-2026.pdf). Knight Frank. JP Morgan's 2.2 million sq ft Powai lease was about 31% of the half-year. ## Transit

- **Metro Line 3 (Aqua), fully open.** Opened end to end on 9 Oct 2025: 33.5 km, 27 stations, Cuffe Parade to Aarey. Weekday average 1.48 lakh riders in Dec 2025; peak day 1.82 lakh. [Source](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/mumbai-metro-3-ridership-more-than-doubles-after-aqua-line-opens-till-cuffe-parade-daily-footfall-hits-new-highs). Marol Naka had the line's highest footfall, 16.45 lakh entries and exits in Oct to Dec 2025, as the interchange with Line 1. Four of the 11 centres and two of the three proposed buildings are within 700 m of it.
- **Metro Line 1 (Blue).** Versova to Ghatkopar through Andheri since 8 Jun 2014; about 5 lakh weekday riders, peak 5.47 lakh; 1.11 billion journeys in 11 years. [Source](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/mumbai-s-first-metro-line-completes-11-years-served-1-11-bn-commuters-125060800465_1.html). Every centre on this atlas is within a kilometre of a Line 1 station: WEH, Chakala, Airport Road, Marol Naka or Saki Naka.
- **Metro Line 7 (Red) at Gundavali.** Dahisar East to Gundavali, open since 20 Jan 2023; a 58 m bridge links Gundavali to the WEH station on Line 1. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gundavali_metro_station). Brings the western suburbs north of Andheri to the WEH cluster (Boston House, Chandak Chambers, Silver Utopia, Zion House).
- **Metro Line 7A to the airport.** The 3.4 km Gundavali to T2 link is 71% built; work stopped in early 2025 over a height clash in the airport funnel and resumed in Oct 2026, with March 2028 the earliest opening. [Source](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/mumbai-news/mumbai-metro-7a-construction-resumes-airports-authority-of-india-clearance-awaited-101791968874324.html). Until then the airport is reached by Line 3 from Marol Naka (one stop to CSMIA T2).
