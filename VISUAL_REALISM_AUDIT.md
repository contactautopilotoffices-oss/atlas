# Visual realism audit: JindalX 3D explorer

23 November 2026, branch `update-jindal-dev`. This is phase 1: what exists, why it reads as
procedural, and what will change. The pilot is Magnus Towers.

## What exists today

| Part | Where | How it is built |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Renderer | `engine/world.js` (plain three.js, not React Three Fiber) | MeshStandardMaterial throughout; ACES tone mapping; Blender sky HDR (`sky.hdr`, sun at azimuth 235°, elevation 24°) for light and reflections; 4096 PCF soft shadows, redrawn on change; GTAO at half resolution; MSAA 4×; a reflection probe for glass; adaptive quality tiers, render on demand (see `PERFORMANCE_REPORT.md`) |
| Diorama per option | `scripts/world/diorama.py` (Blender 5.2 as a Python module, headless) | Overture footprints extruded to estimated heights; each wall is one textured quad strip; parapets, water tanks and stair cabins as boxes; roads, footpaths, kerbs; Blue Line viaduct and stations; exported as one GLB per option with named materials and no images |
| The option building | `generic_option()` in `diorama.py` | A prism of the plot massing: lobby glass band, one tiled facade texture (`opt_glass`), slab-edge bands, a mullion every 1.5 m, a 2.4 m crown, one roof box and four small boxes |
| Facades | `scripts/world/textures.py`, `tex_facades.py` | Painted JPEG facades (window grid baked into colour) with roughness and normal maps from `normals.py`; seven residential/commercial skins picked per building |
| Trees | `protos()` in `diorama.py`; `buildForest()` in `world.js` | Three prototypes: a rounded tree (five spheres of crossed leaf cards), a columnar tree (three stacked spheres), a coconut palm with flat-colour fronds. Two leaf textures (`leaves_a`, `leaves_b`). Instanced, with three levels of detail |
| Tree placement | `prep.py` | Dense in Overture parks, a random band along roads, and a random scatter over all open ground: 6,500 to 20,400 trees per Noida slab |
| Ground | `world.js` `material("ground")` | The roof texture, tinted beige |
| Lighting presets | `engine/andheri-scene-lighting.js` | A time-of-day studio, enabled only for the Flipkart Andheri branding |
| Asset pipeline | `diorama.py --glb`, then gltf-transform weld and meshopt | No texture compression beyond JPEG; no KTX2 toolchain in this container |

There is no Blender MCP connection in this session. Blender runs as the `bpy` 5.2.3 Python
module, headless, as the existing pipeline already does. Poly Haven, ambientCG and the public
CDNs are blocked from this container, so no external CC0 scans can be fetched. Every texture,
tree and model below is generated by scripts in the repository, which also settles licensing:
there are no third-party assets.

## Why it reads as procedural

1. **The option building is a highlighted box.** One facade texture wraps all four sides; there
   is no structure (columns, cores, frames), no depth in the glazing, no entrance architecture,
   no believable roof. At Magnus Towers it is the same as at D1, apart from its size.
2. **The greenery is a carpet.** The random scatter over all open ground puts trees edge to edge
   with no clearings, avenues or lawns. Two leaf textures and two crown shapes repeat 20,000
   times. Palm fronds are flat colour.
3. **The ground has no material.** Between buildings it is the roof texture tinted beige: no
   soil, no grass, no paving, no plot boundaries.
4. **No site.** The option stands on the bare slab: no boundary wall, gate, forecourt, drive,
   parking, planting or lighting. Real Noida office plots all have these.
5. **Context buildings repeat.** Seven facade skins, uniform parapets, one tank type. They read
   correctly at dollhouse distance and as a pattern at street level.
6. **Light is fixed.** One golden-afternoon sun; the time-of-day studio exists but only for
   Andheri. ACES pushes the warm light towards orange and crushes the greens.

## What will change (pilot: Magnus Towers)

### System B: the property showcase

- **A modelled building** (`scripts/world/magnus_hero.py`), built in its own frame and placed on
  the plot massing by the existing `--exterior` hook:
  - stone-clad columns at the ground floor, with a recessed, double-height glazed entrance under a
    cantilevered canopy;
  - unitised curtain wall on floors 1 to 5, with mullions, transoms and spandrel glass;
  - projecting slab-edge bands, a deep framed bay on the road face, aluminium fins, stone-clad
    corner cores;
  - a parapet with coping, and rooftop equipment (lift and stair headrooms, chillers, cooling
    towers, water tanks, a solar array).
- **A site**: boundary wall with railing, gate, guard cabin and boom barrier, a paved forecourt and
  drop-off, an asphalt drive, marked surface parking, a basement ramp, lawns, hedges, shrubs,
  species-varied trees, pole and bollard lights.
- **Materials**: `hero_*` PBR sets (colour, roughness, normal) generated as tileable textures:
  glass with fake interiors (ceiling lights, blinds), spandrel glass, champagne aluminium panels,
  sandstone cladding, exposed concrete, roof membrane, interlocking pavers, asphalt, lawn, mulch.

### System A: the context

- **Trees**: new crowns built from branch clusters of leaf cards, with new leaf atlases (neem-like
  broadleaf, ashoka, palm fronds as cut-out cards, shrubs, bougainvillea), and per-tree height and
  width variation.
- **Placement**: avenue planting at regular spacing along the main roads, dense planting in parks,
  and a thin scatter elsewhere, in place of the carpet.
- **Ground**: a soil-and-patchy-grass material for open ground.
- **Light**: AgX tone mapping (a photographic response that keeps foliage green), and the
  time-of-day studio (morning, midday, golden hour) enabled for JindalX.

## What will not be invented

- **No pool, clubhouse, sports courts or water features.** The workbook does not list them for
  any option.
- **No common cafeteria.** The workbook says the landlord is "planning" one; it is not modelled.
- **The architecture is neutral, not Magnus Greens.** The architect's design (Sanjiv Tandon
  Architects) has not been seen. What is known, and drives the model:
  - B+G+5 and 38,000 sq ft a floor, from the workbook;
  - a glass, glazing and windows contract at C-2, Sector 67, from the Faalcon Concepts BSE filing,
    September 2025.

  Everything else is a plausible neutral design for a new Grade A office in Noida, and is
  documented as such in the model's docstring and in `VISUAL_REALISM_REPORT.md`.
- **Plot elements carry the same caveat.** The site layout, basement ramp position, parking and
  rooftop equipment are typical for such a plot, not surveyed. The position is still the
  indicative massing (see `jindalx/3D-NOTES.md`).

## Performance guard

- **Built the same way.** The hero adds geometry only near the property, shares textures through
  the existing cache, and uses instancing and the forest levels of detail for trees.
- **Measured the same way.** `scripts/world-bench.cjs` runs before and after, from the same
  cameras: triangles, draw calls, frame time, bytes and load.
- **No unexplained regression.** A significant regression is not accepted without investigation.
