# Twin approval checklist

Nothing built by this pipeline is copied to a live `web/models/` folder, or published, until an **approver** has passed it against this list and the founder has said yes. The approver is a reviewer agent started fresh for the job (not the builder). It is given this file, the client's reference photos and the review shots. It is not given the builder's notes or opinions.

What is reviewed is what the client sees:
- the **realtime web viewer**, one shot for every room view the viewer offers (Entry, Feature, Reverse, Detail);
- the Cycles stills, if any are to be shown.

A shot fails if **any** line below fails. When in doubt it fails. The verdict per shot is PASS or FAIL with the line numbers that failed and one sentence each.

## Geometry
1. Nothing floats, sinks, or passes through a wall, glass, door, column or another object.
2. Ceiling lights sit wholly inside one ceiling tile each. No panel crosses a T-bar, and the grid is square to the walls.
3. Wall-mounted items (screens, whiteboards, logos, signage) are level and centred on what they serve: a screen on its table axis, a logo above its desk. Any offset must be visibly deliberate.
4. Ceilings meet walls and glass with no gap, strip or see-through band. Floors meet walls with no gap.
5. Window frames and mullions have one consistent colour and size per facade.
6. Furniture reads as real furniture: chairs have a seat, back, arms and a five-star base, not stacked boxes. Nothing is a crude placeholder.
7. Rooms look plausible: no empty white box where the room type needs furniture or AV.

## Materials
8. No texture is stretched, smeared, streaked or swimming (check the logo letters, wall corners and furniture sides).
9. Walls read as clean matte paint: no mottling, blotches, dirty-marble look or noise.
10. Floors read as the specified finish at true scale (carpet tiles about 600 mm, stone tiles at their size). No camouflage blotches, no z-fighting, no two finishes on one spot.
11. The client's brand is exact: the real wordmark and mark, brand colours as in the reference photos, letterforms crisp.
12. Glass reads as glass: clear, with the frosted band where specified, not grey fog.

## Light
13. Interiors read as a bright, working office in daytime, like the reference photos: white ceilings, lit walls, no dull grey cast.
14. No blown-out areas except the windows; no crushed black areas except true black finishes.
15. No light leaks, black corners, smears at wall edges or columns, or blotchy patches in the baked light.

## Overall
16. Would a client, shown this shot cold, take it for a real office photo or a high-end render? If it looks like a game or a clay model, it fails.

Reference photos for Altruist: `airoli-rtp/brand/altruist-*.jpg` in the main Atlas folder.
